01Scope & our commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how the Bureau of Investments, Ministry of Trade, Investments and Tourism (BOI-MTIT), BARMM handles personal information connected with the BARMM PPP Readiness Dashboard. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

In short: you can use the dashboard without an account. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, we do not sell personal data, and survey results are published only in aggregated form.

BOI-MTIT, BARMM is the personal information controller for the processing described here. Questions and requests go to our Data Protection Officer (DPO):

04The PPP readiness survey data

The dashboard is built from 98 respondent records collected for the PPP readiness baseline. The published platform contains no names, contact details or free-text answers that identify respondents.

  • Results are shown as counts and percentages of valid responses, by survey area and question.
  • Because some area sub-groups are small (for example, four Tawi-Tawi records), we avoid cross-tabulations that could single out an individual, and we do not release respondent-level data.
  • Respondent rights concerning the original survey records may be exercised through the DPO using the contacts above.

05Why we use personal information

  • To answer your questions and route them to the right specialist.
  • To schedule and conduct readiness validation with LGUs and agencies that ask for it.
  • To review and correct reported data errors.
  • To keep the platform secure, available and working properly.
  • To meet legal, audit and records-management obligations of the Bangsamoro Government.

We do not use your information for marketing, profiling or automated decision-making.

06Lawful basis

Under Section 12 of RA 10173 we rely on: your consent, which you give when you submit the contact form; the performance of MTIT’s public functions and mandate under the Bangsamoro Organic Law (Republic Act No. 11054) to promote investment, including PPPs; and our legitimate interest in keeping the platform secure. You may withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect processing already carried out.

07Sharing & service providers

  • Service providers. Netlify, Inc. (hosting and form handling) and Google (font delivery) process limited data on our behalf or to deliver the service.
  • Government partners. Where your inquiry concerns them, we may forward it to the relevant BARMM ministry or office, or to a national agency such as the PPP Center, with only the details needed to respond.
  • Development partners receive survey results in aggregated form only.
  • Legal disclosure occurs only when required by law or a lawful order, and to the minimum extent necessary.

We never sell, rent or trade personal information.

08International processing

Netlify and Google may process data on servers outside the Philippines. MTIT remains accountable for that processing under Section 21 of RA 10173 and relies on the providers’ contractual and security commitments to protect it.

09Security

  • All pages and form submissions use encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • A strict Content Security Policy blocks third-party scripts; the platform loads no analytics or advertising code.
  • Access to contact-form submissions is limited to authorised BOI-MTIT personnel.
  • Assessment Register data never leaves your device.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will act and notify the NPC and affected individuals as required by NPC Circular 16-03.

10Your rights

Under RA 10173 you have the right to be informed; to object; to access; to correct; to erasure or blocking; to data portability; to damages; and to file a complaint. To exercise these rights, email boi@bangsamoro.gov.ph with the subject “Data privacy request”. We may verify your identity first, and we aim to respond within fifteen (15) working days.

11Children

The platform is intended for public officials, professionals, researchers and other adult stakeholders. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform or the law changes. The “Last updated” date shows the current version, and significant changes will be highlighted on the platform.

13Questions & complaints

Please contact our DPO first — most concerns can be resolved quickly. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph or complaints@privacy.gov.ph.
