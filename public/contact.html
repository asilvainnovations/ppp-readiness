Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the dashboard an official rating or ranking of LGUs?

No. The platform is a descriptive evidence baseline drawn from 98 survey respondent records. It does not assign any LGU a PPP maturity score, rank or pass/fail status. Institutional readiness is assessed separately, using documentary evidence, through the six-dimension framework and the Validation Pack.

How representative are the survey results?

The results describe the 98 people who responded across Basilan, Sulu, Tawi-Tawi, Lanao and Maguindanao. They are not a random sample and are not statistically representative of all BARMM LGUs. Percentages use valid responses only, missing answers are never recoded as No, and small area sub-groups should be read with caution.

How can our LGU or ministry take part in readiness validation?

Choose “Validation & LGU participation” in the form below and tell us your LGU or agency, focal person and preferred schedule. The team will share the Validation Pack, explain the documentary evidence requested for each readiness dimension and critical gate, and agree on a review session.

We have a PPP project concept. Can MTIT help?

The Bureau of Investments can advise on early project screening against the critical gates — for example project identification, institutional mandate, fiscal and risk review, and stakeholder engagement — and refer proponents to the appropriate BARMM and national bodies. Project approval follows the Philippine PPP Code (Republic Act No. 11966) and applicable Bangsamoro rules; contacting us is not an application or an endorsement.

Can I use or cite the data?

Yes. Aggregated figures may be quoted for research, policy and media use with attribution. Suggested citation: MTIT–BARMM Bureau of Investments (2026), BARMM PPP Readiness Dashboard, Version 3 working draft. Respondent-level data are not published, to protect respondents’ confidentiality. See the Terms of Service.

I found an error in a figure. How do I report it?

Select “Report a data correction”, name the page and figure (for example, Survey Findings → Heard of PPP), and describe what looks wrong. Every published figure reconciles to the controlling workbook, so we check each report against the source data and correct the dashboard if needed.
