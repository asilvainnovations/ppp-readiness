01About this platform & acceptance

The BARMM PPP Readiness Dashboard (the “platform”), available at ppp-readiness.mtit-barmm.com, presents a descriptive evidence baseline on public–private partnership (PPP) awareness and perceived readiness in the Bangsamoro, a six-dimension institutional readiness framework, critical gates, and a 2027–2035 implementation roadmap.

By opening, browsing, installing or otherwise using the platform you agree to these Terms of Service (“Terms”), together with the Privacy Policy and the Cookie Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

02Who operates the platform

The platform is operated by the Bureau of Investments, Ministry of Trade, Investments and Tourism (BOI-MTIT) of the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM) as part of its PPP Readiness Programme (“MTIT”, “we”, “us”). The analysis was prepared by Alvin M. Silva, MDM. Contact details are in Section 13.

03Nature of the content — please read

In short: the platform describes evidence; it does not rate LGUs, approve projects or give advice.
  • Descriptive, not representative. Survey figures describe 98 respondent records from Basilan, Sulu, Tawi-Tawi, Lanao and Maguindanao. They are not a random sample and are not statistically representative of all BARMM LGUs. Percentages are computed on valid responses; missing answers are never recoded.
  • No rating or ranking. Nothing on the platform is a PPP maturity score, ranking, certification or pass/fail decision about any LGU, ministry or agency. Institutional readiness requires documentary validation.
  • Working draft. Version 3 is a working draft for stakeholder validation. Figures, framework content and roadmap phases may change as evidence is reviewed.
  • Not advice or approval. Content is not legal, financial, procurement or investment advice. It is not an approval, endorsement, award or commitment of government funds, and does not replace the processes required under the Philippine PPP Code (Republic Act No. 11966), its implementing rules, the Bangsamoro Organic Law (Republic Act No. 11054) or applicable Bangsamoro legislation.

04Acceptable use

You may use the platform for lawful research, planning, policy, educational and civic purposes. You agree not to:

  • misrepresent platform content, for example presenting survey percentages as an official rating of an LGU or as representative of the whole region;
  • attempt to re-identify survey respondents or combine platform data with other sources for that purpose;
  • interfere with the platform’s security, availability or performance, including automated scraping at a volume that degrades service;
  • use the MTIT or BARMM seal, name or branding to imply endorsement of a product, project, proposal or organisation;
  • submit unlawful, defamatory, abusive or misleading content through the contact form.

05The Assessment Register (on-device records)

The Assessment Register lets you draft dimension ratings and critical-gate records. These records are stored only in your own browser (localStorage); they are not sent to MTIT and are not an official submission.

  • You are responsible for the accuracy of what you record and for exporting or backing it up — clearing browser data deletes it.
  • Please do not enter personal or confidential information. Records on a shared device may be visible to its other users.
  • To submit evidence formally for validation, use the contact form.
