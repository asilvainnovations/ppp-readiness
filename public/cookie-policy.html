Cookie Policy & Settings

What the BARMM PPP Readiness Dashboard stores in your browser, why, and how to change your choices — no advertising or tracking cookies, ever.

01Summary

No tracking. The BARMM PPP Readiness Dashboard sets no advertising, social-media or cross-site tracking cookies and loads no analytics. It uses a small amount of browser storage so the dashboard works offline, keeps records you choose to save, and remembers your settings.

This policy explains each item stored, why, and how to control it. Read it with the Privacy Policy.

You can also reopen these settings at any time from the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of every page.

03Cookies and browser storage, explained

  • Cookies are small text files a website asks your browser to keep and send back on later visits. This platform does not set any.
  • localStorage keeps small items on your device until you clear them. It is not sent to our servers.
  • sessionStorage works the same way but is erased when you close the tab.
  • Cache Storage (used by the app’s service worker) keeps copies of the platform’s own files so it opens quickly and works offline.

05Third parties

Google Fonts delivers the Montserrat, Poppins and Roboto Condensed typefaces. Your browser contacts Google’s servers to download them, which shares your IP address and browser details; Google states that the Fonts API does not set cookies. Netlify hosts the platform and processes the contact form; it does not set analytics cookies on this site.

06How the consent pop-up works

  1. You can use the platform immediately — nothing optional is switched on before you choose.
  2. After you have spent two (2) minutes on the platform, a Cookie consent & settings panel appears.
  3. Choose Accept all, Essential only, or Customise to switch individual categories on or off.
  4. Your choice is saved on this device and applies to every page. Change it at any time on this page or via “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Strictly necessary storage cannot be switched off because the platform cannot work without it; it is never used to track you.

07Controlling storage in your browser

  • Chrome / Edge: Settings → Privacy and security → Delete browsing data → “Cookies and other site data”.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data → remove ppp-readiness.mtit-barmm.com.

Clearing site data deletes your Assessment Register records — export them first. Private or incognito windows discard storage when closed.

08Changes to this policy

If we add a new storage item or category, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date and, where consent is needed, ask you again.
